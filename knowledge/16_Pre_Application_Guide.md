# Pre-Application & Setup Guide for NY Retail Dispensaries

**Status:** Official Guidance Compilation
**Compliance Standard:** Zero Risk
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-11
**Target Audience:** Prospective Retail Dispensary Applicants

---

## 1. The "Road to Opening" Timeline (T-12 Months)

This timeline provides a structured approach from concept to grand opening.

### Phase 1: Planning & Preparation (Months 1-3)
*   [ ] **Business Entity Formation**: Form an LLC or Corporation in NYS.
    *   *Tip:* Do not use "Cannabis", "Weed", or slang in the legal name if possible to avoid banking flags initially, though "DBA" can differ.
*   [ ] **Assemble Core Team**:
    *   **Attorney**: Must specialize in NY Cannabis Law.
    *   **CPA**: Must understand IRC 280E and NYS Tax Law Article 20-C.
    *   **Compliance Officer**: Designate an internal person responsible for rules.
*   [ ] **Capital Strategy**:
    *   Calculate Startup Costs: $300k - $1M+ (Real estate, renovation, inventory, license fees).
    *   Source Funds: Personal savings, private investors, or NYS Social Equity Investment Fund (if eligible).
*   [ ] **Draft Business Plan**: (See Section 3 below).

### Phase 2: Location & Community (Months 3-6)
*   [ ] **Secure Real Estate**:
    *   **Own or Lease**: Lease must be contingent on license approval.
    *   **Distance Check**:
        *   > 500 ft from Schools.
        *   > 200 ft from Houses of Worship.
    *   **Zoning**: Verify NYC Zoning (C1-C8, M1, etc.) using `NYC Zoning & Land Use Map`.
*   [ ] **Community Board Notification**:
    *   **Mandatory**: Notify the local Community Board **30 days** before filing the OCM application.
    *   (See Section 4 for Notification Template).

### Phase 3: Application & Licensing (Months 6-9)
*   [ ] **Submit Application to OCM**:
    *   Fees: $1,000 Application Fee.
    *   Documents: TPI Disclosures, Lease, Proof of Control, Site Plan.
*   [ ] **Fingerprinting**: All True Parties of Interest (TPI) > 10% ownership.
*   [ ] **Wait for Approval**: Can take 3-6 months depending on queue.

### Phase 4: Build-out & Inspection (Months 9-11)
*   [ ] **Renovation**:
    *   Install Security System (Camera, Vault, Alarms) per 9 NYCRR Part 123.
    *   Secure Certificate of Occupancy (CO) from NYC DOB.
*   [ ] **OCM & FDNY Inspections**:
    *   Schedule Pre-Opening Inspection.
    *   Verify Security/Storage compliance.

### Phase 5: Operations Setup (Month 12)
*   [ ] **Inventory**: Order initial stock from licensed Distributors.
*   [ ] **Tech Stack**:
    *   Install POS (Point of Sale).
    *   Connect to BioTrack (NYS Seed-to-Sale Traceability).
*   [ ] **Staff Training**: Train on ID checks, security, and union rights.
*   [ ] **Grand Opening**: Launch!

## 2. Mandatory Pre-Application Checklists

### A. True Parties of Interest (TPI) Self-Check
*Who really owns the business? OCM requires 100% transparency.*
*   [ ] **Sole Control**: Do you have >51% equity?
*   [ ] **Spouse/Family**: Are they involved? They may count as TPIs.
*   [ ] **Financial Backers**: Anyone loaning money or taking profit share is a TPI.
*   [ ] **Landlords**: If the lease has profit-sharing, they are a TPI (Prohibited in many cases).

### B. "Social and Economic Equity" (SEE) Eligibility
*Check if you qualify for 50% fee reduction and priority review.*
*   [ ] **Minority-Owned Business** (>51%)
*   [ ] **Women-Owned Business** (>51%)
*   [ ] **Distressed Farmer**
*   [ ] **Service-Disabled Veteran**
*   [ ] **Justice-Involved** (You or close family convicted of marijuana offense in NY prior to March 31, 2021).

## 3. NY Cannabis Business Plan Structure (Template)

A generic business plan won't work. Use this OCM-tailored structure.

### I. Executive Summary
*   Mission Statement (Focus on community & compliance).
*   Location summary (Zoning compliant).

### II. Compliance & Operations (Most Important)
*   **Security Plan**: Reference 9 NYCRR Part 123.
    *   *Detail*: "We will use 4k cameras with 60-day storage..."
*   **Youth Prevention**:
    *   *Detail*: "ID Scanners at door + 2nd check at register."
*   **Inventory Control**:
    *   *Detail*: "BioTrack integration SOPs, monthly manual audits."

### III. Community Impact Plan
*   *Requirement*: How will you benefit the neighborhood?
*   *Examples*: Local hiring, supporting local non-profits, keeping street clean.

### IV. Financial Plan
*   **280E Tax Strategy**: Acknowledge federal tax burden.
*   **Cash Management**: Plan for banking (or heavy cash security).
*   **Projections**: 3-year P&L.

## 4. Community Board Notification (Critical Step)

**Requirement:** You must notify the local Community Board (CB) of your intent to apply for a license. The CB has 30 days to issue an "Opinion" (Support, Object, or Neutral).
**Tip:** A negative opinion doesn't automatically kill your application, but it hurts.

### Notification Letter Template

## 5. Banking & Financial Services Guide

**The Problem:** Federal illegality makes traditional banking difficult.
**The Solution:** Use Cannabis-Friendly Financial Institutions (CRBs).

### How to Get a Bank Account
1.  **Do NOT lie**: Never hide the nature of your business. It will lead to immediate account closure and "SAR" (Suspicious Activity Report).
2.  **Target Specific Banks/Credit Unions**:
    *   *Examples (Check current status):* Safe Harbor Financial, certain NYS Credit Unions (e.g., Hudson Valley CU - *verify eligibility*).
3.  **Prepare the "Compliance Packet" for the Bank**:
    *   Copy of OCM License (or application).
    *   Business Plan.
    *   Flow of Funds (Source of capital).
    *   Beneficial Ownership information.

### Cash Management (If Bank is Unavailable)
*   **Smart Safes**: Safes that count cash and credit your account provisionally.
*   **Armored Car Service**: **Mandatory** for moving large amounts of cash for tax payments or deposits. **Do not carry cash yourself.**

## 6. Service Provider Selection Criteria

### A. Security Vendors
*   **Must know Part 123**: Do not use a generic home alarm installer.
*   **Video Retention**: Ensure they guarantee **60-day storage** (OCM requirement).
*   **Perimeter**: Ask about "motion detection" and "glass break sensors".

### B. Architects / General Contractors
*   **Experience**: Have they built a dispensary or pharmacy before?
*   **Secure Storage**: Must understand how to build a **reinforced vault** or secure room.
*   **Flow**: Design for "Check-in Area" (Mantrap) -> "Sales Floor" -> "Secure Exit".
