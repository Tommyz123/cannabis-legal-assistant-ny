## Part 119 Overview

9 NYCRR Part 119 governs all **packaging and labeling** requirements for adult-use cannabis products sold at retail dispensaries in New York State. Compliance is mandatory at the point of sale; products that do not meet these requirements **cannot be sold** and may be subject to recall and penalty.

---

## § 119.1 Definitions

Key terms as defined under Part 119:

- **Lot:** A specific quantity of cannabis product produced in one production run under uniform conditions
- **Batch:** A lot, or a collection of lots from the same production run
- **Universal Cannabis Symbol:** The standardized warning symbol developed by OCM, consisting of an orange exclamation mark in an orange circle on a white background with the text "THC"
- **Child-Resistant Packaging:** Packaging that meets the ASTM D3475 standard (see § 119.3)
- **Serving:** A single recommended dose as designated on the label

---

## § 119.2 General Packaging Requirements

All cannabis products sold at retail dispensaries must be packaged in a manner that:

1. **Is child-resistant** — meets ASTM D3475 standard (see § 119.3)
2. **Is tamper-evident** — shows visible evidence of tampering (see § 119.4)
3. **Is opaque or resealable** — prevents visibility of product contents (see § 119.5)
4. **Does not appeal to persons under 21 years of age** — no cartoon characters, bright colors, or imagery targeting youth
5. **Accurately represents the product** — no deceptive packaging
6. **Does not imitate food, candy, or beverages** — packaging cannot closely resemble non-cannabis consumer products

**Re-Packaging Prohibition:**
Dispensaries may **not** re-package cannabis products. All products must be sold in original manufacturer packaging that has been pre-approved and labeled in compliance with Part 119.

---

## § 119.3 Child-Resistant Packaging

### ASTM D3475 Standard

All cannabis product packaging must comply with **ASTM D3475** (Standard Classification for Child-Resistant Packaging).

**Requirements:**
- Packaging must be **opaque or translucent** (product not visible through packaging)
- Must be **resealable** and maintain child resistance after each opening for products with multiple servings
- Single-use packages (e.g., single-serving edibles) must be child-resistant but do not need to be resealable
- Must maintain child-resistance after opening for products with more than one serving

**Product-Specific Requirements:**

| Product Type | Packaging Requirement |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Edibles (multi-serving) | Child-resistant AND resealable |
| Edibles (single-serving) | Child-resistant (non-resealable acceptable) |
| Flower (>1g) | Child-resistant AND resealable |
| Pre-rolls (multi-pack) | Child-resistant AND resealable |
| Pre-rolls (single) | Child-resistant (non-resealable acceptable) |
| Concentrates | Child-resistant AND resealable |
| Tinctures | Child-resistant closure required |
| Vape cartridges | Child-resistant outer packaging required |

**Exit Packaging (Dispensary Obligation):**
If a customer purchases multiple items, the dispensary must provide an **exit package** that is also child-resistant. Dispensaries must maintain a supply of compliant exit packaging at all times.

---

## § 119.4 Tamper-Evident Requirements

All cannabis product packaging must incorporate **tamper-evident features** that:

- Provide visual evidence of any tampering before reaching the consumer
- Cannot be re-sealed in a tamper-evident manner after opening (i.e., consumer can tell it has been opened)
- Must be clearly distinct from ordinary wear and tear

**Acceptable tamper-evident methods include:**
- Shrink bands or sleeves
- Breakaway caps
- Sealed blister packs
- Induction seals
- Tear strips with holographic indicators

---

## § 119.5 Opaque and Non-Appealing Packaging

### Opacity Requirement
- Packaging must be **opaque** (contents not visible) OR be contained in an opaque outer package
- Exception: transparent or translucent packaging is permitted only if the outer packaging is opaque

### Non-Appeal to Minors Standard
Packaging **must not**:
- Use cartoon characters, toys, or imagery that primarily appeals to persons under 21
- Use bright, primary colors in a manner that appeals to children (e.g., bright candy-like colors)
- Depict a person under 21 years of age
- Imitate or closely resemble the branding of any non-cannabis product primarily marketed to persons under 21
- Use fonts or design elements primarily associated with products marketed to children

---

## § 119.6 Required Labeling Elements

### Mandatory Label Information

Every cannabis product sold at retail must have a label that includes **all** of the following:

1. **Product Name and Type**
   - Common name of the cannabis product (e.g., "Cannabis Flower," "Cannabis Edible," "Cannabis Concentrate")
   - Strain name (if applicable)

2. **License Numbers**
   - OCM license number of the **cultivator** or **manufacturer**
   - OCM license number of the **retail dispensary** (if repackaged at dispensary — note: re-packaging is generally prohibited)

3. **Net Weight or Volume**
   - In both metric (grams/milliliters) and US customary units
   - For edibles: net weight of the entire package

4. **Cannabinoid Content**
   - **THC content:** mg per serving AND mg per package (total)
   - **CBD content:** mg per serving AND mg per package (total, if >0.3% CBD)
   - Label must state both **total THC** and **total CBD** using the formula:
     - Total THC = (THCA × 0.877) + Δ9-THC
     - Total CBD = (CBDA × 0.877) + CBD

5. **Serving Size and Number of Servings**
   - Clearly stated on front of label
   - For edibles: serving size in grams AND mg of THC per serving

6. **Batch or Lot Number**
   - For tracking and recall purposes
   - Must be unique and traceable to batch testing records

7. **Harvest/Production Date** and **Packaging Date**

8. **Expiration Date or Best-By Date** (if applicable)
   - Mandatory for edibles, beverages, tinctures
   - Recommended for all products

9. **Universal Cannabis Symbol**
   - NYS-specific symbol: orange exclamation mark in orange circle with "THC" text
   - Must appear on **front** of package
   - Minimum size: 0.375 inches × 0.375 inches

10. **Allergen Information** (if applicable)
    - Required if product contains any common allergens (nuts, dairy, gluten, etc.)

11. **Storage Instructions**
    - "Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight"
    - Specific refrigeration requirements where applicable

## § 119.7 Mandatory Warning Statements

All cannabis product labels must include **all** of the following warning statements in **legible print** (minimum 6-point font, high-contrast):

### Required Warning Statements (Exact Language):

1. **Age Warning:**
   > "For use only by adults 21 years of age and older. Keep out of reach of children."

2. **Health Warning:**
   > "This product contains cannabis. Cannabis can impair concentration, coordination, and judgment. Do not operate a vehicle or heavy machinery under the influence of this drug."

3. **Pregnancy Warning:**
   > "There may be health risks associated with consumption of this product for women who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant."

4. **General Health Warning:**
   > "WARNING: Use of Marijuana During Pregnancy and Breastfeeding Poses Potential Harms."

5. **Dependency Warning (for products >10mg THC per serving):**
   > "This product may be habit forming."

6. **Edibles-Specific Warning** (required for all edibles):
   > "EDIBLES. Consumption of cannabis-infused products may cause delayed psychoactive effects. Wait at least two hours before consuming additional product."

### Label Format Requirements:
- Warnings must appear on the **outer package** label
- Minimum font size: **6-point** for all warnings
- Must be in **English**; Spanish translation is strongly recommended
- High contrast between text and background (minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio)
- Cannot be obscured by stickers, tape, or other packaging materials

---

## § 119.8 Certificate of Analysis (COA) and QR Code

### COA Requirement
Every cannabis product sold at retail must have an associated **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** from an OCM-licensed testing laboratory, certifying the batch has passed all required tests.

**COA must cover:**
- Cannabinoid potency (THC, CBD, and other cannabinoids)
- Pesticide residues (must pass OCM limits)
- Heavy metals (arsenic, cadmium, lead, mercury)
- Microbial contaminants (Total Yeast and Mold, Total Aerobic Bacteria, E. coli, Salmonella)
- Mycotoxins (for applicable product types)
- Residual solvents (for extracts/concentrates)
- Water activity (for flower and edibles where applicable)

### QR Code Requirement
Each product label must include a **QR code** that links directly to the product's COA.

**QR Code Requirements:**
- Must be scannable and link to the batch-specific COA
- COA must remain accessible online for a minimum of **3 years** after the product's sell-by date
- QR code must be minimum **0.75 inches × 0.75 inches** for readability
- If QR code cannot be placed on primary label, a sticker on secondary packaging is acceptable

**Dispensary Obligation:**
- Must verify that QR codes on all products are functional before placing on shelf
- Must provide alternative access to COA for customers without smartphones upon request

## § 119.9 Maximum THC Content Per Package

OCM establishes maximum THC content per package to limit over-consumption:

| Product Type | Max THC per Serving | Max THC per Package |
|-------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Edibles (solid, e.g., gummies) | **10 mg** | **100 mg** |
| Edibles (beverages) | **10 mg** | **100 mg** |
| Tinctures | **10 mg per dose** | **1,000 mg** |
| Concentrates (vape/wax/oil) | No per-serving limit | **1,000 mg** |
| Cannabis Flower | N/A | No THC limit (by weight) |
| Pre-Rolls | N/A | No THC limit |
| Infused Pre-Rolls | 10 mg per infused serving | **100 mg** (if marketed as edible effect) |

> **Critical for Dispensaries:** Receiving products from manufacturers that exceed these limits is a violation. Verify COA THC content against package limits for all inventory.

---

## § 119.10 Prohibited Label Content

Labels must **not** contain:

- Any health, medical, or therapeutic claims (e.g., "treats anxiety," "reduces pain," "cures insomnia")
- Any statements that cannabis has been approved or evaluated by the FDA
- Statements that appeal to persons under 21
- False or misleading information about product contents, origin, or effects
- Depictions of cannabis consumption (smoking, vaping, eating)
- Celebrity or influencer endorsements (see Part 129 Marketing rules)
- Any logo, mascot, or branding that is primarily associated with products marketed to children
- The word "organic" unless the product has been certified organic by a USDA-accredited certifying agent

---

## Common Labeling Violations and Penalties

Based on OCM enforcement patterns, the following are the most frequently cited labeling violations:

| Violation | Typical Penalty Range |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Missing Universal Cannabis Symbol | $500–$2,000 per item |
| Incorrect or missing THC mg content | $1,000–$5,000 |
| Non-compliant child-resistant packaging | $500–$3,000 + product pull |
| Missing or broken QR code/COA link | $500–$2,000 |
| Label health claim | $1,000–$10,000 |
| Selling product with expired COA | Product seizure + $5,000+ |

## Part 120 — Conditional Adult-Use Retail Dispensary License

### Scope
9 NYCRR Part 120 governs the application, issuance, and renewal of **Conditional Adult-Use Retail Dispensary (CAURD)** licenses.

### CAURD License Overview
- Priority licensing program for **justice-involved individuals** (cannabis-related conviction or immediate family member with such conviction)
- Expedited application process with technical assistance from OCM
- Requirements: business plan, financial documentation, background checks, real estate proof, security plan, employee training program

### Licensing Fees
- Application fee: **non-refundable** (amount set by OCM, check current fee schedule)
- Annual license fee based on license type and business revenue
- Renewal fees due 60–120 days before expiration

### License Renewal
- Applications must be submitted **60–120 days before expiration**
- Late submissions may result in operational gaps or administrative suspension
- Annual renewal required; failure to renew = unauthorized operation

## Packaging Compliance Workflow for Dispensaries

```
Product Received from Licensed Manufacturer
           ↓
Verify manufacturer's OCM license number on label
           ↓
Scan QR code → Confirm COA is accessible and batch-specific
           ↓
Confirm THC/CBD mg per serving and per package within limits
           ↓
Verify child-resistant packaging integrity (not damaged)
           ↓
Confirm Universal Cannabis Symbol present on front label
           ↓
Verify all mandatory warning statements present
           ↓
Confirm no prohibited label content
           ↓
Approve for shelf placement in METRC/BioTrack inventory
```
