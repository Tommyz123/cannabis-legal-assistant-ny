## Part 118 Scope

Part 118 establishes the **definitions** that apply throughout the New York adult-use cannabis regulatory framework (Parts 118-125, 131).

### Key Definition Categories:
1. **License Types** - Cultivator, Processor, Distributor, Retail Dispensary
2. **Product Classifications** - Cannabis flower, concentrate, edible, topical
3. **Operational Terms** - Seed-to-sale tracking, chain of custody, batch, lot
4. **Compliance Terms** - Adverse event, recall, serious adverse event
5. **Business Structure** - Affiliate, applicant, controlling person, licensee

## Critical Definitions for Retail Dispensaries

### Adult-Use Cannabis
Cannabis products intended for consumption by adults 21 years of age or older.

### Retail Dispensary
A licensed entity authorized to sell cannabis products to adult consumers 21+ at a fixed retail location.

### Cannabis Product
Includes:
- Flower (whole/ground)
- Pre-rolls
- Concentrates/extracts
- Edibles (food/beverage)
- Topicals
- Vaporizer cartridges

**EXCLUDES:**
- Medical cannabis (separate regulatory framework)
- Hemp products (regulated under Agriculture & Markets Law)

## Proximity Protected Locations (Part 118 Definitions)

### School Grounds
Building and its grounds occupied exclusively as a school.

**Distance Requirement:** 500 feet
**Measurement Standard (Updated 2024/2025):**
- Measured in **straight line** from dispensary entrance to **nearest point on school property line**
- Previous interpretation (door-to-door on same road) was corrected
- **Temporary standard through February 15, 2026:** Door-to-door measurement applies

**Reference:** https://cannabis.ny.gov/proximity-correction

### House of Worship
Building used primarily for religious services.

**Distance Requirement:** 200 feet
**Measurement Standard:**
- Straight line from center of nearest entrance of worship building to center of nearest entrance of dispensary
