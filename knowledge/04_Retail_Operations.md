## Part 124 - General Business Requirements for Retail Dispensaries

### Scope
Part 124 establishes the core operational requirements for adult-use retail dispensaries, including:
- Business operations standards
- Customer service requirements
- Product handling and sales procedures
- Record-keeping obligations
- Compliance and reporting

---

## Part 125 - General Operating Requirements

### Scope
Part 125 provides detailed operational procedures for retail dispensaries, covering:
- Daily operations
- Staff requirements and training
- Sales limitations and restrictions
- Consumer protection measures

---

## Retail Dispensary Operations (Estimated Requirements)

### Hours of Operation

#### State Requirements:
- Maximum hours set by OCM regulations
- Must post hours of operation prominently
- No sales outside posted hours

#### Local Municipal Authority:
- Municipalities may impose more restrictive hours
- Common restrictions: No sales before 9:00 AM or after 10:00 PM
- Some jurisdictions may prohibit Sunday sales

### Location and Signage

#### Proximity Restrictions:
- **Schools:** 500 feet minimum (measurement standard updated 2024/2025)
  - Temporary door-to-door measurement through February 15, 2026
  - Permanent straight-line measurement from property boundaries after
- **Houses of Worship:** 200 feet (straight-line measurement)

#### Signage Requirements:
- Discrete exterior signage
- No neon or flashing lights
- Universal cannabis symbol required
- Size and placement restrictions
- Must not be visible to minors from public streets (some jurisdictions)

### Age Verification

#### Mandatory ID Check:
- **Minimum Age:** 21 years old
- **Acceptable ID:**
  - Government-issued photo ID
  - Driver's license
  - Passport
  - Military ID
  - NY ID card

#### Verification Procedures:
- Check every customer (no exceptions)
- Scan ID or manual verification
- Document verification in point-of-sale system
- Refuse sale if ID expired, damaged, or suspected fake
- **Zero Tolerance:** Selling to minors results in license suspension/revocation

### Purchase Limitations

#### Individual Purchase Limits:
- **Flower:** Maximum per transaction (typically 3 oz)
- **Concentrates:** Maximum per transaction (typically 24 grams)
- **Edibles:** THC limits per package
- No limit on number of transactions per day (unless locally restricted)

#### Product Restrictions:
- Cannot sell tobacco products
- Cannot sell alcohol products
- Cannabis only (licensed products from licensed suppliers)

## Customer Service and Sales Procedures

### Point of Sale System

#### Required POS Features:
- Integration with NY Seed-to-Sale (BioTrack) system
- Age verification recording
- Purchase limits enforcement
- Real-time inventory deduction
- Receipt generation

#### Transaction Records:
- Customer ID verification (no personally identifiable info stored)
- Products purchased (type, quantity, batch/lot)
- Transaction date and time
- Employee conducting sale
- **Retention:** Minimum 5 years

### Product Display

#### Display Requirements:
- Products locked in secure display cases or behind counter
- Customers cannot handle products directly before purchase
- Samples prohibited (except in some jurisdictions with special approval)
- Product information labels visible

#### Prohibition on Free Samples:
- No free cannabis products
- No promotional giveaways
- No coupons or loyalty programs involving free cannabis

### Consultation and Education

#### Budtender Requirements:
- Product knowledge training required
- Cannot make medical claims
- Provide information on potency, effects, and safe consumption
- Responsible consumption education

#### Required Customer Information:
- Safe storage (keep away from children/pets)
- Start low, go slow (dosage guidance for edibles)
- Do not drive or operate machinery while impaired
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding warnings

---

## Employee Requirements

### Staffing

#### Minimum Staffing Levels:
- At least two employees on premises during operating hours
- One employee must be manager-level or designated responsible person

#### Employee Training (Mandatory):
- Responsible vendor training (RVT)
- Age verification procedures
- Product knowledge
- Security and theft prevention
- Emergency procedures
- Seed-to-sale tracking system
- Customer service

### Employee Registration:
- Background check required
- OCM employee registration
- Badge/ID required while on duty
- Training documentation maintained

---

## Prohibited Activities

### Sales Prohibitions:
- No sales to persons under 21
- No sales of non-cannabis products (except branded merchandise)
- No delivery sales (separate license type required)
- No consumption on premises (unless special on-site consumption license)

### Marketing and Advertising Restrictions:
- No advertising targeting minors
- No unsolicited marketing materials
- No billboards within 1,500 feet of school
- No radio/TV advertising between 6 AM - 10 PM
- No celebrity endorsements or testimonials
- Social media restrictions

---

## Compliance and Record-Keeping

### Required Records:
- Employee records (training, background checks)
- Inventory records (receipt, storage, sale)
- Security system records (video, alarm logs)
- Transaction records
- Waste disposal records
- Financial records

### Record Retention:
- Minimum 5 years for most records
- 7 years for financial records
- Available for OCM inspection

### Reporting to OCM:
- Monthly inventory reports
- Quarterly financial reports
- Incident reports (within 24 hours)
- License renewal applications (60-120 days before expiration)

---

## Inspections and Audits

### OCM Inspection Authority:
- Unannounced inspections allowed
- Access to all areas of premises
- Review of all records
- Video surveillance review
- Employee interviews

### Compliance Violations:
- Minor violations: Citation and corrective action plan
- Serious violations: Fines, suspension, or revocation
- Criminal violations: Referral to law enforcement
