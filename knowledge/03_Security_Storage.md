## Part 121 - Adult-Use Cannabis (General Framework)

### Scope
Establishes the overall framework for adult-use cannabis regulation, including:
- License types and classifications
- Operational standards
- Security and safety requirements
- Compliance and enforcement mechanisms

---

## Part 123 - Licensing Requirements

### Scope
Detailed licensing procedures and requirements for all cannabis license types, with specific provisions for retail dispensaries.

## Security Requirements (Estimated from Parts 121, 123)

### Physical Security Systems

#### Video Surveillance (24/7 Recording Required)
- **Camera Coverage:**
  - All entrances and exits
  - Sales floor and customer areas
  - Storage and inventory areas
  - Cash handling locations
  - Loading/receiving areas
  - Perimeter of building

- **Recording Standards:**
  - Minimum resolution requirements
  - 24/7 continuous recording
  - Retention period: Minimum 45 days
  - Footage available for OCM inspection
  - Timestamps on all recordings

- **System Specifications:**
  - Fixed cameras (not movable by staff)
  - Backup power supply for power outages
  - Secure storage for recordings
  - Tamper-proof system

#### Alarm Systems
- **Required Systems:**
  - Intrusion detection system
  - Motion sensors in storage areas
  - Door and window sensors
  - Panic buttons at strategic locations
  - 24/7 monitoring by licensed security company

- **System Testing:**
  - Regular testing schedule
  - Documentation of all tests
  - Maintenance records

#### Access Control
- **Limited Access Areas:**
  - Inventory storage rooms
  - Cash handling areas
  - Restricted to authorized personnel only

- **Access Control Methods:**
  - Electronic key card systems
  - Biometric access (recommended)
  - Access logs maintained
  - Visitor log required

### Building Security

#### Structural Requirements
- Secure doors and locks
- Reinforced entry points
- Bars or security film on windows (if required by local jurisdiction)
- Safe or vault for cash storage

#### Lighting
- Adequate exterior lighting
- Motion-activated lights recommended
- Illumination of entrances and parking areas

## Storage and Inventory Requirements

### Seed-to-Sale Tracking System

**System Name:** NY Seed to Sale (STS) / BioTrack
**Implementation:** November 2024 (mandatory integration)

#### Tracking Requirements:
- Real-time inventory updates
- Track all cannabis products from receipt to sale
- Batch and lot tracking
- Waste disposal tracking
- Transfer manifests for all product movements

### Inventory Storage

#### Storage Area Requirements:
- **Separate and Secure:**
  - Locked storage room(s)
  - Limited access (authorized personnel only)
  - Climate controlled (temperature and humidity)
  - Video surveillance coverage

- **Inventory Organization:**
  - Clear labeling of all products
  - Batch and lot segregation
  - FIFO (First In, First Out) system
  - Expired products segregated

#### Daily Inventory Reconciliation:
- Physical inventory count procedures
- Discrepancy investigation protocols
- Documentation of all inventory movements
- Regular audits

### Product Handling

#### Receiving Procedures:
- Verify transport manifest
- Inspect products for damage or tampering
- Enter into tracking system immediately
- Quarantine until verification complete

#### Disposal Procedures:
- Approved waste disposal methods
- Rendering cannabis unusable (grinding + mixing with waste)
- Documentation of all disposal
- Video recording of disposal process

## Personnel Security

### Background Checks
- Required for all employees with access to cannabis
- Fingerprinting required
- Criminal history review by OCM
- Disqualifying offenses specified in regulations

### Employee Identification
- ID badges required at all times
- Visible to customers and inspectors
- Include name, photo, and license number

### Training Requirements
- Security procedures training
- Emergency response protocols
- Theft prevention training
- Documentation of all training

---

## Transportation Security

### Product Transport Requirements
- Licensed distributor only (retailers cannot self-distribute)
- Secure, enclosed vehicles
- GPS tracking on all vehicles
- Transport manifest required
- Direct route (no stops except delivery points)

### Transport Manifest
- Origin and destination information
- Product details (batch, lot, quantity)
- Estimated departure and arrival times
- Driver identification
- Vehicle identification

---

## Cash Management

### Cash Handling Security
- Secure safe or vault on premises
- Time-delay safes recommended
- Limited cash on sales floor
- Armored car service for bank deposits (recommended)

### Financial Records
- Separate accounting system
- Track all cash transactions
- Daily reconciliation
- Records available for OCM inspection

---

## Emergency Procedures

### Required Plans:
- **Emergency Response Plan:**
  - Fire evacuation procedures
  - Medical emergency protocols
  - Natural disaster response

- **Security Incident Response:**
  - Robbery or theft procedures
  - Immediate notification to law enforcement
  - OCM notification within 24 hours
  - Incident documentation
