  - Expedited application process
  - Lower application fees

#### Standard Retail Dispensary License:
- Open application periods
- Competitive review process
- Higher application fees

### 2. Location Requirements and Proximity Restrictions

#### State-Level Requirements (9 NYCRR):

**Schools - 500 Feet:**
- Measured from dispensary entrance to nearest point on school property
- **CRITICAL UPDATE (2024/2025):** Measurement standard changed
  - Original intent: Straight-line measurement
  - Incorrect interpretation: Door-to-door on same road only
  - **Temporary Standard (until Feb 15, 2026):** Door-to-door measurement accepted
  - **Permanent Standard (after Feb 15, 2026):** Straight-line from property boundaries

**Houses of Worship - 200 Feet:**
- Measured from center of nearest entrance of worship building to center of nearest entrance of dispensary
- Straight-line measurement

#### Municipal Authority:
- Municipalities can impose additional restrictions
- Cannot prohibit dispensaries entirely (state preemption)
- Can regulate hours, signage, and additional distance requirements

## Proximity Correction - Critical Update (2024/2025)

### Background:
OCM regulations require 500-foot distance from schools. Initial regulatory language was interpreted inconsistently.

### Correction Issued:
**Original Interpretation (Incorrect):**
- Door-to-door measurement
- Only if on same road
- Led to some dispensaries being approved too close to schools

**Corrected Interpretation (Correct):**
- Straight-line measurement (as originally intended)
- From dispensary entrance to nearest point on school property line
- Applies regardless of road configuration

### Transition Period:
- **Through February 15, 2026:** Door-to-door measurement remains valid for existing applications and approvals
- **After February 15, 2026:** Only straight-line measurement accepted
- **Existing Dispensaries:** Grandfathered if approved under old measurement standard before correction

**Source:** https://cannabis.ny.gov/proximity-correction

## Detailed License Application Process and Timeline

### Application Types Overview

OCM offers multiple retail license types, each with different application processes and timelines.

#### 1. CAURD (Conditional Adult-Use Retail Dispensary) License

**Target Applicants:**
- Justice-involved individuals (prior cannabis convictions)
- Non-profit organizations serving justice-involved communities

**Advantages:**
- Expedited application process
- Reduced application fees
- Priority review
- Business support and technical assistance

**Application Requirements:**
- Proof of cannabis conviction or non-profit eligibility
- New York State residency (applicant or key personnel)
- Business plan and financial documentation
- Proof of site control (lease or purchase agreement)
- Community impact plan

**Timeline:**
- Application submission: Open application period (check OCM announcements)
- Initial review: 30-60 days
- Background check: 60-90 days
- Final approval: 3-6 months (total)

---

#### 2. Standard Adult-Use Retail License

**Target Applicants:**
- All eligible applicants
- Open competitive review process

**Application Requirements:**

**A. Business Entity Documents:**
- Articles of incorporation (LLC, Corp, Partnership, etc.)
- Disclosure of all True Parties of Interest (TPI)
- Organizational chart

**B. Personal Background Checks:**
- Personal history disclosure for all TPIs
- Fingerprinting and background investigation
- Financial disclosure and proof of funding sources

**C. Site and Location Documents:**
- Proof of site control (deed or lease agreement)
- Retail dispensary floor plan
- Distance compliance documentation:
  - 500 feet from schools (straight-line, after February 15, 2026)
  - 200 feet from houses of worship
  - Distance from other retail dispensaries (population > 20,000: 1,000 ft; population < 20,000: 2,000 ft)
- Municipal zoning compliance certification

**D. Operational Plan:**
- Security plan (video surveillance, alarm system, access control)
- Operating procedures (inventory management, sales process, employee training)
- Community impact plan

**E. Financial Documents:**
- Business plan
- Financial statements and tax returns
- Proof of funding sources
- At least 6 months of operating capital documentation

**Application Timeline:**
- Application preparation: 2-4 months (recommended)
- Application submission: Open application period
- Initial eligibility review: 60-90 days
- Background check: 90-120 days
- Site and security review: 30-60 days
- Final approval: **6-12 months total**

---

#### 3. Provisional vs. Final License

**Provisional Approval:**
- Applicants may apply without detailed site information (if intending to sell own cannabis products)
- After OCM confirms eligibility, applicant receives provisional approval
- 12 months to submit remaining site information and documentation
- Converts to final license upon site submission

**Final License:**
- Includes all site, security, and operational documentation
- Requires Certificate of Occupancy from NYC Department of Buildings (NYC DOB)
- If selling non-cannabis products, requires Certificate of Authority from NYS Department of Tax and Finance

### Detailed Step-by-Step License Application Guide

#### Step 1: Due Diligence and Preparation (2-4 months before application)

**A. Business Structure Decision:**
- Choose business entity type (LLC, S-Corp, C-Corp)
- Register business in New York State
- Obtain Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN)

**B. Site Selection and Investigation:**
- Use OCM distance map tool: https://cannabis.ny.gov/proximity-protected-location-map
- Verify zoning compliance (contact municipal planning department)
- Secure site control (lease or purchase)
- Conduct environmental site assessment

**C. Financial Preparation:**
- Ensure at least 6-12 months of operating capital
- Prepare financial statements (past 3 years)
- Document all funding sources
- Open business bank account

**D. Team Assembly:**
- Identify all TPIs (owners, senior management, key employees)
- Hire key roles: Compliance Officer, Security Manager, Operations Manager
- Retain professional advisors: cannabis attorney, accountant, architect

---

#### Step 2: Application Document Preparation (1-2 months before application)

**Required Document Checklist:**

**Business Entity Documents:**
- Certificate of incorporation
- Articles of organization or operating agreement
- TPI disclosure forms (all owners and senior management)
- Organizational chart

**Personal Documents (each TPI):**
- Personal history disclosure form
- Government-issued ID copy
- Resume/personal statement
- Financial disclosure

**Site Documents:**
- Lease agreement or property deed
- Floor plan (showing all areas: sales, storage, office, restrooms)
- Distance measurement proof (schools, houses of worship, other dispensaries)
- Zoning compliance letter (from municipality)

**Operational Plan:**
- Business plan (market analysis, competitive analysis, financial projections)
- Security plan (video surveillance, alarm, access control, cash management)
- Inventory control plan (BioTrack/Metrc integration strategy)
- Employee training plan
- Emergency procedures (fire, medical emergencies, security incidents)

**Community Impact Plan:**
- Employment plan (local hiring, diversity commitments)
- Community engagement strategy
- Environmental sustainability measures

**Financial Documents:**
- Business and personal tax returns (past 3 years)
- Bank statements (past 6 months)
- Proof of funding sources (loan agreements, investor agreements)
- Financial projections (3 years)

---

#### Step 3: Online Application Submission

**Submission Platform:**
- OCM licensing portal: https://cannabis.ny.gov/licensing
- Create account and complete online application

**Application Fees:**
- CAURD: Reduced fees (check current rates)
- Standard retail license: $2,000 - $5,000 (application fee, non-refundable)
- Annual license fee: $4,000 - $20,000 (depending on population and scale)

**After Submission:**
- Receive confirmation email (application number)
- Application status trackable online
- OCM may request supplemental documentation (typically within 30 days)

---

#### Step 4: Background Check and Review (3-6 months)

**Background Check Process:**
- All TPIs must be fingerprinted
- OCM coordinates with New York State Police (NYSP) and FBI
- Review of criminal records, financial history, integrity
- **Duration:** 60-120 days

**Background Check Considerations:**
- Certain criminal records may result in automatic disqualification (violent crimes, major drug trafficking)
- Cannabis-related convictions generally do not disqualify (especially for CAURD applicants)
- Financial violations (tax fraud, money laundering) may result in denial

**Site and Security Review:**
- OCM reviews site compliance (distances, zoning)
- Security plan review (camera coverage, alarm systems)
- Possible on-site inspection

**Financial Review:**
- Verification of funding source legitimacy
- Assessment of financial sustainability
- Review of tax compliance history

---

#### Step 5: License Approval and Conditional License (if approved)

**Approval Notification:**
- OCM issues license approval letter
- Lists any conditions or requirements
- Provides license number

**Conditional License Status:**
- Status between approval and opening
- Must complete:
  - Site construction/renovation
  - Security system installation
  - BioTrack/Metrc system integration
  - Staff recruitment and training
  - Inventory procurement preparation

**Critical Requirements (before opening):**
- Obtain Certificate of Occupancy (CO) - NYC DOB
- Complete BioTrack/Metrc integration and testing
- Pass OCM pre-opening inspection
- Obtain any local permits (DCWP, health department, etc.)

## BioTrack/Metrc Seed-to-Sale System Integration

### System Overview

**Current Transition Status (2026):**
- **Legacy System:** BioTrack THC (used 2024-2025)
- **New System:** Metrc (statewide deployment beginning 2026)
- **Requirement:** All licensees must maintain real-time inventory tracking

**Key Concepts:**
- Seed-to-sale tracking: Complete product lifecycle tracking from cultivation to final retail sale
- API integration: Third-party software transmits data to OCM systems via API
- Package UID (Package Unique Identifier): Unique identifier for each product package
- Retail Item UID: Unique identifier for each retail unit

---

### Metrc Transition Timeline (2026)

**Key Deadlines:**

| Date | Requirement | Impact |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **2026-01-12** | Retail facilities must purchase Package UIDs and associate all existing inventory to Package UIDs | Unassociated inventory cannot be sold |
| **2026-02-28** | Product transfers must include Retail Item UIDs | Distributors cannot transfer products without UIDs to dispensaries |
| **2026-03-31** | All retail inventory must have test results (TestPassed or RetestPassed) | Products without test results must be removed from shelves |

**Retail ID QR Code Requirements:**
- As part of the Metrc seed-to-sale system, Retail ID QR Codes will be required on final cannabis product packaging
- Meets regulatory requirements under Part 128.5(b)(9) and 128.5(b)(10)

---

## Pre-Opening Inspection Process

### OCM Pre-Opening Inspection Overview

**Purpose:**
- Verify facility compliance with all state regulations
- Confirm security systems are operational
- Verify BioTrack/Metrc integration
- Ensure staff have been trained
- Authorize opening

**Timing:**
- Must be completed before opening
- Typically scheduled after all construction and system installation is complete
- Inspection usually takes 2-4 hours

---

### Pre-Inspection Preparation Checklist

**A. Facility and Construction (30 days before inspection)**

**Certificate of Occupancy (CO):**
- Obtain final CO from NYC DOB
- CO must state legal use and permitted occupancy
- Ensure CO covers retail cannabis sales

**Site Preparation:**
- All construction and renovations complete
- Clean and organized (no clutter or safety hazards)
- All areas clearly labeled: sales, storage, office, restrooms
- ADA accessibility compliance

**Signage and Labels:**
- Exterior signage compliant (no neon or flashing lights)
- Includes universal cannabis symbol
- 21+ entrance signage
- Business hours clearly posted
- No on-site consumption signage

---

**B. Security Systems (14 days before inspection)**

**Video Surveillance System:**
- Cameras installed at all entrances/exits, sales areas, storage areas
- High-definition, color, sufficient to identify individuals
- Minimum 60-day storage capacity
- Test all cameras and retrieve sample footage
- Submit screenshots of all cameras (showing coverage areas)

**Alarm System:**
- Installed and monitored by licensed central monitoring station
- Submit letter from monitoring station confirming all system components are installed, operational, and transferred to monitoring
- Test alarm (intrusion, fire, panic button)
- Backup power (UPS or generator) - minimum 8 hours

**Access Control:**
- Locking systems for restricted areas (storage, office)
- Access log system (electronic or paper)
- Secure storage (vault or safe room) for inventory

**Security Equipment Inspection:**
- All security equipment inspected every 30 days (maximum interval)
- Retain inspection records (5 years)
- Provide recent inspection logs

---

**C. Inventory and Tracking System (7 days before inspection)**

**BioTrack/Metrc Integration:**
- POS system fully integrated and tested
- Demonstrate real-time inventory deduction
- Show sales transaction data flow
- Provide test transaction records

**Initial Inventory:**
- If inventory has been received, all products must be in Metrc
- All Package/Retail UIDs scanned and recorded
- Products correctly categorized and labeled
- Certificates of Analysis (COA) on file

**Inventory Storage:**
- Products securely stored in locked areas
- Climate control (temperature/humidity)
- Product segregation: compliant vs. needs relabeling vs. damaged

---

**D. Operational Documents (7 days before inspection)**

**Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):**
- Age verification SOP
- Sales transaction SOP
- Inventory receiving and storage SOP
- Inventory audit SOP
- Waste disposal SOP
- Security incident response SOP
- Emergency evacuation SOP

**Employee Records:**
- Background checks completed for all employees
- Employee training certificates (covering all required topics)
- Employee badges or identification
- Employee roster and job descriptions

**Vendor and Distributor Agreements:**
- Agreements with licensed vendors only
- Product sourcing and COA verification process

---

**E. Health and Safety (7 days before inspection)**

**Handwashing Facilities:**
- Handwashing sinks accessible to all employees
- Soap, paper towels, hand sanitizer

**Restrooms:**
- Clean, functional restrooms
- In compliance with health codes

**Ventilation and Lighting:**
- Adequate ventilation in all areas
- Adequate lighting (sales, storage, security areas)

**Pest Control:**
- Contracted regular pest management service
- Provide service agreement

---

### OCM Inspection Process (Inspection Day)

**Phase 1: Document Review (30-60 minutes)**

Inspector will review:
- Certificate of Occupancy (CO)
- Security plan and system documentation
- Employee training records
- SOPs and operations manual
- Vendor agreements
- Proof of insurance

**Preparation Tips:**
- Organize all documents in a binder or digital folder
- Prepare copies (inspector may request to retain)
- Designate a knowledgeable person (Compliance Officer or Manager) to accompany inspector

---

**Phase 2: Facility Walk-Through (30-60 minutes)**

Inspector will check:
- All areas (sales, storage, office, restrooms)
- Security camera coverage and function
- Alarm system and backup power
- Product storage and security
- Cleanliness and organization
- Fire safety (extinguishers, exits, emergency lighting)

**Common Inspection Points:**
- Do cameras cover all blind spots?
- Are products locked and accessible only to authorized personnel?
- Is all signage clear and compliant?
- Are handwashing and restroom facilities functional?

---

**Phase 3: System Demonstration (30-60 minutes)**

Inspector may request demonstration of:
- **Age Verification:** Scan ID, show POS verification
- **Sales Transaction:** Complete simulated sale, show Metrc update
- **Inventory Query:** Locate specific product in Metrc
- **Video Retrieval:** Retrieve and play back past footage
- **Alarm Test:** Trigger alarm, show monitoring station response

**Preparation Tips:**
- Practice demonstrations in advance
- Ensure all systems are fully functional before inspection day
- Have backup equipment ready (in case of technical failure)

---

**Phase 4: Staff Interviews (15-30 minutes)**

Inspector may interview staff about:
- Age verification procedures
- Sales restriction knowledge
- Security protocols (alarm, panic button)
- Metrc system usage
- Emergency procedures

**Preparation Tips:**
- Conduct mock interviews with all staff
- Ensure staff are familiar with all SOPs
- Emphasize honesty (if unsure, say "I'll check the SOP" rather than guessing)

---

**Phase 5: Final Review and Approval (15-30 minutes)**

**Possible Outcomes:**

**Pass (Unconditional):**
- Inspector approves opening
- Provides written approval letter or inspection report
- Opening date can be scheduled immediately

**Conditional Pass:**
- Approved to open, but minor issues must be corrected
- Provided corrective action list and deadlines
- Follow-up inspection or document submission required after opening

**Fail:**
- Significant compliance issues found
- All issues must be corrected
- Inspection must be rescheduled
- Opening delayed

**Common Reasons for Failing:**
- Insufficient video surveillance (blind spots, inadequate storage capacity)
- BioTrack/Metrc integration not functional
- Security system failures (alarm, backup power)
- Insufficient staff training
- Missing critical documents (CO, insurance, employee records)

---

### Post-Inspection Follow-Up

**If Passed:**
1. Receive formal opening approval
2. Set opening date
3. Notify staff, vendors, community
4. Final inventory receiving
5. Open!

**If Conditionally Passed:**
1. Immediately address identified issues
2. Submit proof of corrections before deadline
3. Complete any required follow-up inspections
4. Receive final approval

**If Failed:**
1. Review inspection report and all violations
2. Develop detailed corrective action plan
3. Implement all required corrections
4. Reschedule inspection (may incur additional fees)
5. Re-prepare and re-practice

### Common Application Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

#### Mistake #1: Incomplete or Inaccurate TPI Disclosure

**Problem:**
- Failure to disclose all True Parties of Interest
- Inaccurate or outdated financial information
- Concealing criminal history

**Consequences:**
- Application denial
- Possible fraud charges
- Permanent disqualification

**How to Avoid:**
- Fully disclose all owners, investors, senior management
- Provide accurate financial information (with supporting documents)
- Honestly disclose criminal history (cannabis convictions generally do not disqualify)
- Have attorney review before submission

---

#### Mistake #2: Distance Requirement Violations

**Problem:**
- Site too close to school or house of worship
- Incorrect distance measurement method

**Consequences:**
- Site rejection
- Application delay (must find new site)
- Loss of lease or purchase agreement

**How to Avoid:**
- Use OCM distance map tool before signing lease or purchase agreement
- Conduct professional measurement (hire a surveyor)
- Understand current measurement standards:
  - Schools: straight-line distance (after February 15, 2026)
  - Houses of worship: straight-line (center-to-center)
- Maintain buffer margin (e.g., if 500 feet required, seek 550+ feet)

---

#### Mistake #3: Insufficient Funding Source Documentation

**Problem:**
- Unable to prove legitimacy of funding sources
- Unrealistic financial projections
- Insufficient operating capital

**Consequences:**
- Financial review failure
- Application denial
- Delay (waiting for additional documentation)

**How to Avoid:**
- Document all funding sources (bank statements, loan agreements, investor agreements)
- Provide at least 6-12 months of operating capital proof
- Prepare realistic financial projections (conservative revenue estimates)
- Consult a cannabis-specialized accountant

---

#### Mistake #4: Inadequate Security Plan

**Problem:**
- Insufficient video surveillance coverage
- No backup power plan
- Lack of detailed security procedures

**Consequences:**
- Security plan rejection
- Pre-opening inspection failure
- Opening delay

**How to Avoid:**
- Hire a security consultant or professional installer
- Over-comply (exceed minimum requirements)
- Include detailed camera layout diagram
- Designate 8+ hours of backup power
- Detailed access control and cash management procedures

---

#### Mistake #5: Missing Application Windows or Deadlines

**Problem:**
- Missing open application period
- Failing to submit complete application before deadline
- Not responding to OCM information requests in time

**Consequences:**
- Must wait for next application cycle
- Application denial (if incomplete)
- Delayed approval

**How to Avoid:**
- Subscribe to OCM email updates
- Check OCM website regularly (weekly)
- Set deadline reminders (30 days in advance)
- Prepare early (do not wait until last minute)
- Respond promptly to all OCM requests (typically 15-30 days)
